Effective [EFFECTIVE DATE] · Applies to the Kharcha apps for iPhone and Android and this website

The TL;DR

  • Everything you log in Kharcha is stored only on your phone.
  • We don't run a server that receives your entries, and we can't see them.
  • There's no account, no bank connection, no SMS access and no advertising.
  • You can export or erase your data whenever you like.

1. What we collect

Kharcha is made by [COMPANY NAME] ("we", "us"). We do not collect, receive or store the expenses, amounts, notes, categories, budgets or payment methods you enter in the app. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools in the app, and we do not build a profile of you.

2. What stays on your phone

The app saves your entries, budget, favourite categories and settings in its own private storage on your device. Your phone's operating system may include this storage in your device backups (iCloud or Google backup), depending on your phone's settings; those backups are handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies, not by us.

If you delete the app or erase your data from inside it, that data is removed from the app on your device. We have no copy we could restore.

3. Exports and sharing

When you export your entries or share a Month Wrapped card, the app creates a file or image and hands it to whichever app or person you choose. Once shared, it is governed by that app's or person's practices. We never receive a copy.

4. Permissions

Kharcha does not ask for access to your SMS, contacts, location, camera, microphone or bank accounts. It only uses your phone's standard share and file pickers when you choose to export, import or share.

5. App stores, this website and email

Apple's App Store and Google Play may share aggregated, anonymised statistics with us, such as download counts and crash rates, under their own terms. This website does not use advertising cookies or trackers. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply and keep a record of the conversation, and we delete it on request.

6. Children

Kharcha is not directed at children under 13, and because the app collects nothing about its users, we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the EU and UK GDPR, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your personal data, and to complain to a regulator. Your app data is already fully in your hands: you can view, edit, export and erase it in the app. For anything we hold, such as an email you sent us, write to the address below.

8. Changes and contact

If we ever change how Kharcha handles data, for example by adding an optional backup, we will update this page and tell you in the app before the change applies to you.
